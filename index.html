Questions

Before you ask.

Does this replace my agents? +
No. Front Desk AI handles the first reply and organizes the enquiry — your team still handles the relationship, the negotiation, and closes the deal.
Does it work on WhatsApp too? +
Front Desk AI is built to embed directly on your website. You can also share your assistant's link over WhatsApp so customers can chat with it there, even without a full WhatsApp Business integration.
Does it know when a property is sold? +
It works off the listing information you give it. If you mark a property as sold or unavailable, it tells customers that instead of booking a viewing — it only knows what you keep updated.
How long does setup take? +
Usually 24–48 hours once we have your listing details — property type, location, price, and a short description for each one.
What does it cost? +
We start every agency with a free trial on one listing, no cost and no obligation, so you can see it working before anything else is discussed.
