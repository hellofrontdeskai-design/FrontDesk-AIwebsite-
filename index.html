Clear answers

Questions an owner should ask before saying yes.

Does FrontDesk AI replace our agents?+

No. It handles the first response, repetitive questions, information capture, and routing. Your agents handle negotiations, inspections, offers, and relationship-building.

Will it invent property prices or availability?+

No. It only uses information supplied and approved by your company. When something is missing or uncertain, it routes the question to a human instead of guessing.

Can it book an inspection?+

It collects the property, preferred date, preferred time, and contact details. Your agent or company confirms the actual inspection time.

How does the 3-day live trial work?+

Day one is setup and approval. Day two is testing and launch. Day three is a real conversation review and results report.
