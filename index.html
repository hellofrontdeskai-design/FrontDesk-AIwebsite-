FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Front Desk AI replace my agents? +
No. It handles the initial conversation and repetitive questions. Your team remains responsible for sales, negotiation, and relationship-building.
Can it use our actual properties? +
Yes — the system is configured around your company's property information and approved knowledge.
What happens when it doesn't know the answer? +
It shouldn't invent one. The conversation is handled according to your configured escalation process.
Can we update our properties? +
Yes — your system is kept up to date as listings, prices and availability change.
Can customers request inspections? +
Yes, if your business has an inspection process configured — the AI collects the details and passes the request to your team.
Can it work after office hours? +
Yes — that's one of the main reasons to have an AI receptionist. Your website doesn't need to "close" when your office does.
Will customers know they're talking to AI? +
Yes. The goal isn't to trick customers — it's to give them a useful first response while human help remains available.
What happens after the AI captures a lead? +
It's passed into your configured lead-management workflow so your team can follow up.
Do I have to change my entire website? +
No — Front Desk AI works as an additional layer on your existing website.
